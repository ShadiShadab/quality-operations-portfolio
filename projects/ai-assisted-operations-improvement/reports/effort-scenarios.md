# Effort scenarios (expected benefit, not results)

> **Synthetic data.** Generated by `src/generate_data.py` (fixed seed). These figures demonstrate the method; they are not measurements of any real organisation.


This is a **scenario model**. It combines lane counts from the held-out rules evaluation with assumed handling times. It shows what would have to be true for the redesign to be worth it, and which assumption matters most. No AI model was run, so the AI rows depend entirely on an assumed acceptance rate.


| Assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Manual triage, per request | 4.0 min |
| Spot-check of an AUTO-routed request | 0.5 min |
| REVIEW with an AI suggestion the coordinator accepts | 1.5 min |
| REVIEW without a usable suggestion | 4.0 min |
| PROTECTED requests | always manual |

Held-out split: 311 requests, of which 37 PROTECTED.

| Scenario | Triage minutes | Change vs current | Error exposure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current: everything triaged by hand | 1244 | — | — |
| Policy A: rules decide alone (not recommended) | 495 | -60% | 17 silent misroutes |
| Policy B: rules + web-form agreement, no AI | 1006 | -19% | 0 silent misroutes |
| Policy B + AI suggestions, 40% accepted | 800 | -36% | unknown: accepted-but-wrong rate not measured |
| Policy B + AI suggestions, 60% accepted | 697 | -44% | unknown: accepted-but-wrong rate not measured |
| Policy B + AI suggestions, 80% accepted | 594 | -52% | unknown: accepted-but-wrong rate not measured |

## Reading the scenarios

- Policy A saves the most time on paper but sends requests to the wrong queue without telling anyone. That is a worse outcome than a slow, correct one.
- Policy B is slower but, on this data, made no silent misroutes. The saving comes from structured intake, not from AI.
- AI only acts on the REVIEW lane. Its value depends on two numbers a pilot must measure: how often coordinators accept the suggestion, and how often an accepted suggestion turns out to be wrong.
- Triage effort is a small part of the picture: about four hours a week in the simulated baseline. The waiting time before first touch and for missing information affects requesters more, and the intake form and protected lane address those without AI.
