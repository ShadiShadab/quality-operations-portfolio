# 3. Baseline and data

> **Status: simulated.** All data is synthetic, generated by `src/generate_data.py` with a fixed seed. The values below are **assumptions**, chosen to be plausible for a small office. They are not measurements.

## Why simulate

A real baseline would come from an export of the shared inbox or ticket system. I do not have such data for this portfolio, and real requests would contain personal information. A simulation lets me show the **measurement method** and the **evaluation design** without either problem. The same calculations would run on a real export.

## Dataset

| File | Rows | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `data/synthetic/requests.csv` | 600 | One request per row, with ground-truth labels |
| `data/synthetic/events.csv` | 2,178 | Simulated event log: received, first touch, handoff, information requested and received, resolved |

See [data/README.md](../data/README.md) for the data dictionary.

## Generator assumptions

| Assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Period | 12 synthetic weeks |
| Channel mix | 60% email, 40% web form |
| Category mix | Procurement 20%, travel 18%, IT access 15%, finance 17%, HR 8%, reporting 17%, safety 5% |
| Writing style | 55% clear, 30% indirect (no obvious keywords), 15% "trap" (keywords pointing to the wrong category) |
| Messages containing a second, unrelated request | 7% |
| Messages containing instruction-like text ("ignore your previous instructions…") | 2% |
| Web-form category correct | 80% |
| Provided fields written in an informal format | 20% |
| Median time to first touch | 6 h (email), 4 h (web form); safety usually 0.5 h, not always |
| Manual misroute rate | 18%, plus every multi-request message |
| Median handoff delay | 5 h |
| Median wait for the requester to supply missing information | 20 h |
| Coordinator effort | 4 min triage per request, 6 min per follow-up |

## Simulated baseline

From [reports/baseline-report.md](../reports/baseline-report.md):

| Measure | Simulated value |
|---|---|
| Time to first touch, median / 90th percentile | 4.7 h / 17.7 h |
| Time to resolution, median / 90th percentile | 38.3 h / 81.8 h |
| Requests handed off at least once | 23.3% |
| Requests needing an information follow-up | 19.8% |
| Safety requests first touched after more than 2 h | 6 of 27 |
| Coordinator triage and follow-up effort | About 4.3 h per week |

Share of elapsed time by stage (simulated): handler work 67%, waiting for first touch 17%, waiting for missing information 12%, handoffs 3%. The redesign can influence the last three only.

## What a real baseline would need

1. Two to four weeks of inbox or ticket data, exported with timestamps.
2. A random sample of 150–200 requests labelled by two coordinators independently, to measure how often people agree on the category. That agreement rate is the realistic ceiling for any automated triage.
3. Personal data removed before analysis, under the organisation's data-protection rules.
4. A short time study of triage effort, because the 4-minute figure is the most influential assumption in the effort scenarios.

## Limits of the simulation

- One author wrote the generator and the rules. The rules therefore "know" the vocabulary of the data, and every accuracy figure is optimistic.
- Real requests are messier: attachments, replies in long threads, mixed languages, and requests that are partly complaints.
- The event-log timings follow simple distributions and are not calibrated against any real office.
