# Simulated current-state baseline

> **Synthetic data.** Generated by `src/generate_data.py` (fixed seed). These figures demonstrate the method; they are not measurements of any real organisation.


The event log is produced from the assumptions listed in [docs/03-baseline-and-data.md](../docs/03-baseline-and-data.md). The value of this report is the **measurement method**: the same calculations would run on a real export of a mailbox or ticket system.

## Where the elapsed time goes

Each interval between consecutive events is attributed to the stage it ends. Calendar hours, summed over all requests.

| Stage | Share of elapsed time | Addressed by the redesign? |
|---|---|---|
| Handler work (last step before resolution) | 67% | No: outside the scope of intake and triage |
| Waiting for first touch | 17% | Yes: triage order, protected lane, AUTO routing |
| Waiting for the requester to supply missing information | 12% | Yes: required-field form, completeness check |
| Handoffs between queues | 3% | Yes: better routing, one request per submission |

## How to read this

- Most elapsed time is handlers doing the work. Intake and triage changes cannot shorten that, so claims about end-to-end speed must be modest.
- Of the time the redesign can influence, waiting for first touch and waiting for missing information are the largest parts. Neither needs AI to improve.
- The safety figure is the most important row. In the current design, an urgent request waits in the same inbox as a stationery order.
